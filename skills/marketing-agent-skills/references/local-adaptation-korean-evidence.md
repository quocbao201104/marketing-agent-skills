# Local Adaptation Evidence — Korean Addressee Speech-Level Realization

Scope: evidence for `KO-LANG-SPEECH-01`.

This ledger is deliberately narrow. It does **not** establish a Korea market profile, a business-etiquette guide, a hierarchy engine, a universal Korean politeness scale, or a marketing-effect prescription. Evidence here supports only bounded Korean addressee speech-level realization and the boundaries needed to keep that mechanism from absorbing ordinary Korean honorific grammar.

Keep:

```text
ADDRESSEE SPEECH LEVEL
!= SUBJECT HONORIFICATION
!= OBJECT / ACTION-TARGET HONORIFICATION

FORMAL / NON-FORMAL REGISTER
!= ONE MONOTONIC RESPECT SCALE

ATTESTED STYLE SHIFTING
!= EVERY MIXED DRAFT IS VALID

FIRST-PARTY ORGANIZATION POLICY
!= POPULATION-WIDE LANGUAGE RULE

ADVERTISING DISTRIBUTION
!= EFFECTIVENESS

FAVORABLE AD ATTITUDE
!= CONVERSION / REVENUE

LANGUAGE-ETIQUETTE GUIDANCE
!= EXECUTABLE HIERARCHY RULE
```

## [KOLA01] National Institute of Korean Language — `상대 높임법` sentence-ending speech levels

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `상대 높임 표현과 비격식체` (2026-06-15).

**URL:** https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&qna_seq=332328

**Type:** current official Korean-language advisory citing dictionary definitions.

**Supports:**

- `상대 높임법` is realized through sentence-final endings directed at the addressee.
- The system includes `해라체`, `하게체`, `하오체`, `하십시오체`, `해체`, and `해요체`.
- `해요체` and `해체` are both inside the addressee-honorification system.

**Does not establish:**

- one universal brand preference;
- a customer-to-ending lookup;
- equal current use of all six styles in every commercial context;
- a global commercial ranking of the six styles.

## [KOLA02] National Institute of Korean Language — formal/non-formal style is not identical to respect level

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `격식체의 해라체` (2025-05-12).

**URL:** https://m.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=314653

**Type:** official Korean-language advisory citing dictionary definitions.

**Supports:**

- `격식체` includes `해라체`, `하게체`, `하오체`, `하십시오체` and is described in ceremonial/direct/assertive/objective terms.
- `비격식체` includes `해체`, `해요체` and is described as softer/more subjective.
- Because both classes contain different addressee-honorification values, formal/non-formal cannot safely be collapsed into one higher-respect/lower-respect axis.

**Does not establish:**

- `격식체` as a universal professional-marketing default;
- `비격식체` as universally friendlier or more persuasive;
- artifact-type-to-style rules.

## [KOLA03] National Institute of Korean Language — same-listener formal/non-formal shifting can be legitimate

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `해요체와 하게체를 섞어 쓰면 어색한가요?` (2026-06-12).

**URL:** https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=27&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=332226

**Type:** current official Korean-language usage advisory.

**Supports:**

- One speaker may use formal and non-formal addressee styles with the same listener depending on the concrete situation.
- Relationship and situation both matter to `상대 높임법` realization.
- Mixed formal/non-formal use cannot be declared wrong without context.

**Does not establish:**

- every mixed draft being intentional, natural, or valid;
- ignoring approved organization or surface policy;
- arbitrary switching among all speech levels;
- a marketing-effect rule.

## [KOLA04] 2022 news-interview study — `하십시오체 ↔ 해요체` style shifting in formal situations

**Source:** `뉴스 인터뷰를 활용한 한국어 상대 높임법 교육 방안 연구`, *학습자중심교과교육연구* 22(24), 2022, 885–902.

**URL:** https://scholar.kyobobook.co.kr/article/detail/4010036993180

**DOI:** 10.22251/jlcci.2022.22.24.885

**Type:** published Korean-language applied-linguistics / Korean-language-education study.

**Supports:**

- The study analyzes `하십시오체 ↔ 해요체` style shifting in formal news-interview situations.
- It reports frequent shifting in the interview sample and argues that a simple formal/non-formal textbook dichotomy is insufficient for natural relative-honorific use.
- Supported style shifting can therefore be a real realization pattern rather than automatic inconsistency.

**Does not establish:**

- every formal interview needing both styles;
- a universal switching frequency;
- marketing effectiveness;
- organization-specific voice policy.

## [KOLA05] National Institute of Korean Language — subject, object, and addressee honorification are distinct mechanisms

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `높임표현의 유래` (2025-07-11).

**URL:** https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=261&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=318012

**Type:** official Korean-language grammatical advisory citing dictionary definitions.

**Supports:**

- `주체 높임법` concerns the sentence subject and commonly uses `-시-`.
- `객체 높임법` concerns the target/object of an action and includes lexical forms such as `뵙다`, `드리다`, `여쭈다`.
- `상대 높임법` concerns the addressee and is realized through sentence endings.
- These dimensions should not be collapsed into one politeness score.

**Does not establish:**

- a separate runtime unit for every Korean honorification mechanism;
- a hierarchy graph or rank resolver;
- population or marketing preference.

## [KOLA06] National Institute of Korean Language — object honorification can coexist with an addressee-directed ending

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `상대 높임` (2024-12-02).

**URL:** https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=306904

**Type:** official Korean-language grammatical advisory.

**Supports:**

- In `이 책을 아주머니께 가져다 드려라`, lexical `드리다` realizes object honorification toward an action target while the clause separately uses an addressee-directed `해라체` ending.
- Object/action-target honorification and addressee speech level can coexist without being the same decision.

**Does not establish:**

- a universal rule for choosing `드리다`;
- speaker/listener hierarchy from the form alone;
- a separate Korean object-honorification runtime owner.

## [KOLA07] KB customer-language guide — one organization uses different speech styles by communication function

**Source:** KB의 생각, `고객의 신뢰를 얻는 KB고객언어 가이드`.

**URL:** https://kbthink.com/main/customerservice/kbcommunication.html

**Type:** current first-party organization/customer-language policy.

**Supports:**

- KB explicitly varies tone by purpose, target, and situation.
- Its scoped guidance uses `하십시오체` for policy/notices, terms, objective data, expert/investment guidance, thanks, and error/refusal/restriction contexts.
- It uses `해요체` for requests, recommendations, action prompts, difficult explanations, and asking customer intent.
- It separately uses nouns/nominal endings for bounded surfaces such as titles, banners, notes, and buttons.
- One organization may legitimately resolve different styles by function/surface rather than one global customer relationship rule.

**Does not establish:**

- a Korean-population preference;
- a banking-industry rule;
- `policy → 하십시오체` or `CTA → 해요체` universally;
- marketing effectiveness.

## [KOLA08] Ahn, Lee & Chung 2015 — TV-ad distribution and non-final constructions

**Source:** 안의진, 이철우, 정휘만 (2015), `TV광고언어에서 격식체와 비격식체의 사용빈도: 관여도, 문장유형, 발화맥락을 중심으로`, *한국광고홍보학보* 17(3), 232–260.

**URL:** https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002016155

**DOI:** 10.16914/kjapr.2015.17.3.232

**Type:** KCI-indexed Korean advertising content analysis.

**Supports:**

- The study analyzed 1,750 TV-ad sentences.
- Formal/non-formal ending frequencies differed by product involvement, sentence type, and articulation context in the sample.
- 57.6% of analyzed TV-ad sentences were non-final constructions without a formal/non-formal sentence ending.
- Marketing surfaces can therefore contain no material addressee speech-level decision at all.

**Does not establish:**

- a current universal Korean-advertising norm;
- conversion or causal effectiveness;
- `high involvement → formal` as a prescription;
- transfer from TV ads to all channels.

## [KOLA09] Ahn 2018 — bounded consumer-response effect of formal/non-formal ad endings

**Source:** 안의진 (2018), `격식체, 비격식체 광고언어의 소비자 반응 효과`, *광고학연구* 29(5), 81–100.

**URL:** https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002368494

**DOI:** 10.14377/KJA.2018.7.15.81

**Type:** KCI-indexed advertising experiment study.

**Supports:**

- The paper reports two experiments comparing formal and non-formal Korean ad endings.
- In the tested conditions, non-formal endings produced more favorable attitudes toward the ad than formal endings.
- The reported effect was larger for radio than print in the second experiment.
- Speech-level choice can affect audience response under bounded conditions.

**Does not establish:**

- `해요체` always outperforming formal endings;
- conversion, revenue, or purchase causality beyond measured outcomes;
- a universal channel rule;
- a 2026 population default;
- a default runtime recommendation.

## [KOLA10] National Institute of Korean Language — `사물존대` / indirect subject-honorification boundary

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `사물존칭인지 확인 부탁드립니다` (2025-10-20).

**URL:** https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=73&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=322418

**Type:** official Korean-language grammatical usage advisory.

**Supports:**

- Where `객실` itself is the subject, forms such as `객실이 없으세요`, `4인실이세요`, and `객실이 남아있으세요` are treated as inappropriate honorification of the room.
- Where the customer is the understood subject, forms with `-시-` may be appropriate.
- Indirect subject honorification can involve closely related body parts, properties, possessions, or similar relations, and analysis can differ.

**Does not establish:**

- `inanimate + -시- → always wrong`;
- `customer-related noun + -시- → always correct`;
- a customer-service correction lookup;
- promotion of this mechanism inside `KO-LANG-SPEECH-01`.

## [KOLA11] National Institute of Korean Language — `압존법` is not a general workplace/social hierarchy rule

**Source:** 국립국어원 온라인가나다, `압존법 관련 문의` (2026-06-26).

**URL:** https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=73&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=332830

**Type:** current official Korean-language etiquette advisory citing `표준 언어 예절`.

**Supports:**

- `압존법` is not fixed by Korean language regulations.
- `표준 언어 예절` describes traditional use mainly in family and teacher-student contexts.
- A general broadcast/public/workplace/social relative-rank suppression rule cannot be assumed.

**Does not establish:**

- banning all `압존법`-like usage;
- a listener-rank/referent-rank algorithm;
- a hierarchy graph;
- any marketing-effect claim.

## Runtime synthesis boundaries

The runtime unit may rely on the following bounded synthesis:

```text
TARGET LANGUAGE = KOREAN
+
ADDRESSEE SPEECH-LEVEL REALIZATION IS MATERIALLY OPEN OR BEING CHANGED
+
THE CHOICE CAN ALTER ADDRESSEE RESPECT / REGISTER /
INTERPERSONAL DISTANCE / DISCOURSE FUNCTION
→ KO-LANG-SPEECH-01 MAY APPLY
```

```text
FORMALITY REQUEST
→ DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY CHANGE ADDRESSEE RELATIONSHIP OR RESPECT LEVEL
```

```text
SUPPORTED SAME-LISTENER SPEECH-LEVEL SHIFT
→ DO NOT NORMALIZE MERELY FOR SURFACE CONSISTENCY
```

```text
MIXED SPEECH LEVELS WITHOUT SUPPORT
→ MAY BE REPAIRED BACK TO RESOLVED STATE
```

```text
ONLY SUBJECT / OBJECT HONORIFICATION IS OPEN
→ OUTSIDE KO-LANG-SPEECH-01
```

```text
SURFACE NATURALLY HAS NO FINITE ADDRESSEE ENDING DECISION
→ NO LOAD
```

Keep these boundaries explicit:

```text
KOREAN
!= MAXIMUM HONORIFICATION

CUSTOMER
!= 하십시오체

MARKETING
!= 해요체

FORMAL
!= MORE RESPECTFUL

FRIENDLY
!= 해체

BUSINESS
!= 하십시오체 BY DEFINITION

SAME ORGANIZATION
!= ONE SPEECH LEVEL FOR EVERY SURFACE / FUNCTION

ADDRESS-NEUTRAL / NON-FINAL WORDING EXISTS
!= MUST USE IT
```

No evidence in this ledger justifies a Korea country pack, a second Korean honorification unit, a hierarchy resolver, a speech-level score, a new route, a new owner, a Chapter 07 change, controller changes, `routing-index.json` changes, or Korean-specific loader code.
