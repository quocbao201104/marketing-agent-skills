# Condensed abstracts

This reference contains high-density principle abstracts, negative boundaries, and completion criteria for the 22 specialist namespaces indexed by [routing-index.json](../routing-index.json).

It provides an immediate, bounded fallback for environments without Python helper execution (such as browser-based ChatGPT and Claude.ai interfaces), or when an agent requires rapid cross-domain invariant checks without loading multi-thousand-word chapters.

The governing rules from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) apply across all namespaces:
- `observation ≠ interpretation ≠ hypothesis ≠ decision`
- `qualitative recurrence ≠ population prevalence`
- `attribution ≠ incrementality ≠ causality`
- `platform name ≠ reason to load that platform`
- `must appear ≠ must lead`
- `must be prominent / early ≠ primary value / core promise`

# Part I: Copywriting decision runtime

## Namespace: `copywriting`

**Governing path:** `handbook/04-messaging-proof-and-copy.md`  
**Route families:** `copywriting.core`, `copywriting.persuasion`, `copywriting.angle`, `copywriting.progression`, `copywriting.closure`, `copywriting.craft`, `copywriting.editing`, `copywriting.short-form`, `copywriting.handoffs`.

### Core mechanism
Copywriting is an execution layer over adopted strategy, not a substitute for strategy. Nine independent entry points address specific friction points in expression:
- *Diagnosis (`.persuasion`)*: Identifies the reader's resistance envelope (indifference, skepticism, risk, friction).
- *Angle (`.angle`)*: Chooses the communicative frame and hook without inventing unsupported claims.
- *Progression (`.progression`)*: Arranges claims and evidence in logical dependency order before asking for commitment.
- *Closure (`.closure`)*: Binds proof to the requested action without artificial urgency or manipulative friction.
- *Craft & Editing (`.craft`, `.editing`)*: Enhances clarity, cadence, and voice fidelity without inflating scope.
- *Short-form (`.short-form`)*: Compresses arguments to format constraints while preserving relationship and claim boundaries.

### Material boundaries
- `PROSE EXCELLENCE ≠ STRATEGIC VALIDITY`
- `MUST APPEAR ≠ MUST LEAD`
- `SUPPORTING PROOF ≠ ORGANIZING PREMISE`
- `CLEAR COPY ≠ PERSUASIVE COPY`
- `CONCRETENESS ≠ UNFILTERED SPECIFICATION DUMP`
- `CREATIVE EXPLORATION ≠ PERMISSION TO FABRICATE EVIDENCE`

### Completion criteria
The delivered copy artifact expresses the adopted message and proof, directly resolves the diagnosed resistance state, respects character/format limits, preserves truthful product constraints, and passes the audience-facing content-selection gate without filler.

# Part II: Channels and distribution architecture

## Namespace: `content`

**Governing path:** `handbook/08-content-environments-and-distribution.md`  
**Route families:** `content.core-grammar`, `content.meaning-representation`, `content.audience-interaction`, `content.governance-eligibility`, `content.machine-mediation`, `content.feedback-dynamics`, `content.measurement-evidence`, `content.invariants`, `content.fast-path`, `content.consequential-strategy`, `content.job-measurement`, `content.performance-diagnosis`, `content.platform-adaptation`.

### Core mechanism
Distribution operates through a six-layer model: Human Meaning → Audience State → Governance Eligibility → Machine Mediation (recommenders) → Feedback Dynamics → Measurement Evidence. Content is an object with typed edges (actor, topic, audience, intent).

### Material boundaries
- `HUMAN MEANING ≠ MACHINE MEDIATION`
- `DISTRIBUTION ≠ ENGAGEMENT ≠ COMMERCIAL INTENT`
- `REPUTATION / PARTICIPATION ≠ VIRAL REACH`
- `SURFACE ENGAGEMENT METRIC ≠ CAUSAL INCREMENTALITY`
- `PLATFORM MENTION ≠ REASON TO LOAD PLATFORM PLAYBOOK`

### Completion criteria
Artifact aligns with designated distribution layer; typed actor/audience edges remain explicit; descriptive metrics remain separate from incrementality or causal claims.

### Material boundaries
- `VISUAL HIERARCHY ≠ INFORMATION DEPENDENCY`
- `SCROLL DEPTH ≠ COMPREHENSION`

- `HERO PROMINENCE ≠ PRIMARY VALUE PROPOSITION`

### Completion criteria

## Namespace: `email`

**Governing path:** `handbook/12-email-communication-architecture.md`  
**Route families:** `email.core`, `email.send-decision`, `email.send-state`, `email.sequence`, `email.allocation`, `email.continuity`, `email.observation`, `email.decision-record`, `email.invariants`.

### Core mechanism
Email operates as an asynchronous state machine governing the three-way send choice: `SEND`, `WAIT`, or `SUPPRESS`. Decisions depend on recipient relationship history, envelope permission, and inbox message allocation.

### Material boundaries
- `SEND DECISION ≠ BROADCAST VOLUME`
- `OPEN RATE ≠ READER ATTENTION` (Apple MPP proxy image prefetching contaminates raw opens)
- `UNSUBSCRIBE RATE ≠ PASSIVE FATIGUE`
- `SEND FREQUENCY ≠ PERSUASIVE IMPACT`
- `PERSONALIZATION TOKEN ≠ AUTHENTIC RELATIONSHIP`

### Completion criteria
Explicit predicates for send/wait/suppress; telemetry analysis isolates proxy artifacts; narrative sequence preserves cross-surface continuity with landing pages.

# Part IV: Social and content platform environments

## Namespace: `facebook`
**Governing path:** `platforms/facebook.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Multi-environment family (Feed, Groups, Pages). Groups require local community norms and reciprocal value; Feed is governed by graph-based and AI recommendation algorithms.
- **Boundaries:** `GROUP PARTICIPATION ≠ ORGANIC FEED ADVERTISING`; `SURFACE ENGAGEMENT ≠ AUDIENCE REACH`.
- **Completion criteria:** Representation matched to target surface; group posts deliver discussion value before commercial promotion.

## Namespace: `instagram`
**Governing path:** `platforms/instagram.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Multi-surface visual distribution (Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore). Message density and intent vary by surface: Stories (retention/depth), Reels (top-of-funnel interest graph), Feed (identity/curation).
- **Boundaries:** `REEL VIEW DURATION ≠ COMMERCE INTENT`; `AESTHETIC POLISH ≠ CONVERSION VALUE`.
- **Completion criteria:** Asset format and narrative progression match viewer expectation on the specific surface.

## Namespace: `linkedin`
**Governing path:** `platforms/linkedin.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Professional identity and workplace graph. Value centers on industry relevance, operational insight, and career reputation. Personal profiles drive organic commentary; Company Pages serve verification and recruiting.
- **Boundaries:** `NETWORK CONNECTION COUNT ≠ DECISION-MAKER ATTENTION`; `PERSONAL OVER-SHARING ≠ PROFESSIONAL TRUST`.
- **Completion criteria:** Content structured around inspectable business expertise and professional discussion.

## Namespace: `tiktok`
**Governing path:** `platforms/tiktok.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Sequential interest graph. Distribution is determined by immediate hook retention (0–3s), completion rate, and topical engagement signals, largely independent of follower count.
- **Boundaries:** `VIRAL TREND MIMICRY ≠ BRAND AFFINITY`; `TOTAL VIEWS ≠ INCREMENTAL REVENUE`.
- **Completion criteria:** Hook, sound, pacing, and visual pattern match interest-graph discovery dynamics without fabricating urgency.

## Namespace: `x`
**Governing path:** `platforms/x.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Real-time conversational network and For You feed driven by distinct prediction heads (reply, repost, like, bookmark, click). Fast velocity and high contextual decay.
- **Boundaries:** `ENGAGEMENT BAITING ≠ THOUGHT LEADERSHIP`; `ALGORITHM FORMULA HUNTING ≠ AUDIENCE VALUE`.
- **Completion criteria:** Concise argument, punchy hook, and clear participation invitation suitable for conversational threading.

# Part V: Commerce platform environments

## Namespace: `commerce`
**Governing path:** `handbook/09-commerce-environments-and-product-discovery.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Multi-stage shopper journey (Availability → Discovery → Selection → Evaluation → Purchase). Separates durable product catalog identity from platform-specific commercial listings.
- **Boundaries:** `SEARCH SELECTION CARRIER ≠ PDP EVALUATION CARRIER`; `CATALOG IDENTITY ≠ LISTING TITLE`; `QUERY MATCHING ≠ REVENUE CONVERSION`.
- **Completion criteria:** Factual product information allocated to appropriate carriers (title for search/browse, bullets/specs for evaluation).

## Namespace: `google-commerce`
**Governing path:** `platforms/commerce/google-shopping.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Structured Merchant Center product feed, Search/Shopping SERP placements, Gemini multimodal shopping results, and UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol) checkout.
- **Boundaries:** `FEED INGESTION ≠ IMPRESSION ELIGIBILITY`; `CONVERSATIONAL SHOPPING ATTRIBUTES ≠ KEYWORD STUFFING`.
- **Completion criteria:** Feed attributes conform to Google requirements; conversational attributes support entity-matching in AI Overviews.

## Namespace: `amazon`
**Governing path:** `platforms/commerce/amazon.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Shared ASIN catalog model with Featured Offer (Buy Box) dynamics. Strict title character length rules (July 2026 update). Separation of visible customer copy and hidden search terms.
- **Boundaries:** `SELLER LISTING INPUT ≠ AMAZON-RENDERED DETAIL PAGE`; `BACKEND SEARCH TERMS ≠ BULLET POINT COPY`.
- **Completion criteria:** Titles adhere to category limits; bullet points focus on customer benefits; search terms contain non-redundant synonyms.

## Namespace: `tiktok-shop`
**Governing path:** `platforms/commerce/tiktok-shop.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Shoppable video, live commerce, and marketplace showcase. Creator affiliate linkages and product relinking mechanics.
- **Boundaries:** `VIDEO VIRALITY ≠ SKU STOCK READINESS`; `CREATOR CLAIM ≠ SELLER POLICY RECORD`.
- **Completion criteria:** Product link anchor confirmed; SKU variant availability verified before promotional creative commitment.

## Namespace: `shopee`
**Governing path:** `platforms/commerce/shopee.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Southeast Asian mobile-first marketplace. Complex variations, shop tier badges (Mall, Preferred), voucher stackability, and buyer search sorting.
- **Boundaries:** `TIER BADGE STATUS ≠ ORGANIC ALGORITHM PROMOTION`; `DISPLAYED PROMOTIONAL PRICE ≠ NET SELLER MARGIN`.
- **Completion criteria:** Variant taxonomy cleanly configured; final price accountings reflect voucher and fee economics.

## Namespace: `etsy`
**Governing path:** `platforms/commerce/etsy.md`  
- **Core mechanism:** Marketplace for handmade, vintage, and craft supplies. Dual-phase query matching (CSR) and ranking (neural retrieval). Strict listing title and 13-tag allocation rules.
- **Boundaries:** `TAG OVERLAP IN TITLE ≠ SEARCH RANKING LIFT`; `AI TITLE SUGGESTIONS ≠ VALIDATED RANKING SIGNALS`.
- **Completion criteria:** All 13 multi-word tags utilized; titles natural and readable; listing attributes fully populated.

- **Boundaries:** `VENTURE RULES ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE`; `CONTENT QUALITY SCORE ≠ ORGANIC SALES CONVERSION`.
