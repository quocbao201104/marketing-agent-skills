# Research Methodology

Status: **RESEARCH COMPLETE — FOUNDATIONAL METHODOLOGY / REPOSITORY GOVERNANCE**

This directory defines the scientific and architectural research standards governing how Marketing Practitioner capabilities, theories, and evidence models are investigated, adversarially reviewed, and promoted into the runtime package.

The methodology enforces a research-first, evidence-oriented, falsification-seeking, and minimality-driven approach: every substantive change must originate from a concrete, demonstrated failure or boundary ambiguity, rather than speculative expansion or desired tables of contents.

## Governing Question

> What standards of evidence, falsification pressure, and architectural minimality must a marketing theory or runtime abstraction satisfy before promotion into the practitioner decision engine?

Key assumptions rejected during scoping:
- Building an agent skill begins by accumulating the largest possible inventory of marketing frameworks.
- Plausible theoretical prose or popular industry consensus is sufficient to justify runtime authority.

## Disposition

```text
STATUS: FOUNDATIONAL GOVERNANCE STANDARD
RUNTIME TARGET: None (Repository-level governance standard)
GOVERNS: All research tracks under research/* and promotional reviews
```

- **Runtime Target**: None directly; governs pre-promotion research discipline and repository boundaries.
- **Mechanisms Added**: Concrete failure-chain framing (`INPUT → CURRENT → FAILURE → WHY IT CHANGES DECISION → SMALLEST CORRECTION`), 4-tier change risk classification, and post-review repair ledgers.
- **Expansions Rejected**: Universal taxonomy building, ungrounded cognitive architectures, and speculative domain expansions.

## Protected Invariants

```text
PROVENANCE / EVIDENCE LEDGER != RUNTIME INSTRUCTION
EMPIRICAL RECURRENCE != CAUSAL TRUTH
CORRELATION != CAUSATION
ATTRIBUTION != INCREMENTALITY
QUALITATIVE RECURRENCE != POPULATION PREVALENCE
STATING WILLINGNESS-TO-PAY != OPTIMAL SUSTAINABLE PRICE
```

- Never promote a new controller job, primitive, or specialist chapter when existing primitives can represent the decision.
- All substantive claims must cite primary or authoritative evidence and state both what the evidence supports and what it does not support.
- Research artifacts preserve history, exploration, and rejected hypotheses; they are never bundled into distributable agent skills.
