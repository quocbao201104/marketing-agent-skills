# Local Adaptation Extensions Research

Status: **RESEARCH COMPLETE — FOUNDATIONAL METHODOLOGY / REPOSITORY GOVERNANCE**

This directory preserves the research design freeze and adversarial review briefs governing the **architecture, promotion criteria, and boundary conditions for local adaptation extensions** across Marketing Agent Skills.

The research establishes strict governance rules for international and regional specialization: local adaptation modules are strictly subordinate to Handbook Chapter 07 (International Marketing and Ethics). They do not serve as generic "country profiles", "cultural defaults", or independent decision owners, but rather provide bounded evidence specializing an already-open linguistic or commercial decision.

## Governing Question

> Under what architectural constraints and empirical gates may language- or market-specific adaptation modules be integrated into the skill without fragmenting shared theory or converting cultural stereotypes into rigid defaults?

Key assumptions rejected during scoping:
- Every target country requires an autonomous cultural profile or distinct marketing handbook.
- Local adaptation modules own top-level marketing strategy, positioning, or pricing decisions.
- Mentioning a country or language in a prompt automatically justifies overriding core marketing principles.

## Disposition

- **Runtime Target**: `skills/marketing-agent-skills/adaptations/` and Chapter 07 routing bindings.
- **Mechanisms Added**: Promotion gate criteria, relationship-realization interfaces, and negative cultural stereotyping boundaries.
- **Expansions Rejected**: Monolithic country guidebooks, demographic caricature taxonomies, and ungrounded cultural scoring rubrics (e.g., rigid Hofstede stereotyping).

## Protected Invariants

```text
EXISTING DECISION OWNER != SCOPED LOCAL ADAPTATION EVIDENCE
LOCAL RELEVANCE != PERMISSION TO REOPEN RESOLVED STATE
COUNTRY / MARKET NAME != AUTOMATIC ADAPTATION ACTIVATION
LOCAL UNIT = SPECIALIZES ALREADY-OPEN DECISION (NOT A NEW OWNER)
SHARED THEORY REMAINS SHARED
```

- Local adaptation units specialize an open decision owned by a handbook chapter; they do not become new decision owners.
- Upstream decisions (positioning, value proposition, pricing architecture) remain settled and cannot be reopened merely because a regional context is introduced.
- A local module activates only when target-language realization or localized commercial mechanics materially alter execution.
