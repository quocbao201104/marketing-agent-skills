## [KOLA01] National Institute of Korean Language — `상대 높임법` includes six sentence-ending speech levels

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `상대 높임 표현과 비격식체` (2026-06-15).  
https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&qna_seq=332328

**Evidence type**  
Current official Korean-language advisory citing dictionary definitions.

**Supports**

- `상대 높임법` is realized by choosing sentence-final endings that index the addressee.
- The system includes `해라체`, `하게체`, `하오체`, `하십시오체`, `해체`, and `해요체`.
- `해요체` and `해체` are both part of addressee honorification rather than being outside the system.

**Does not support**

- one brand should use all six styles;
- one universal mapping from customer type to ending;
- a claim that every speech level is equally current in every marketing context;
- a global scalar that ranks all six styles on one commercial preference axis.

## [KOLA02] National Institute of Korean Language — formal vs non-formal style is not identical to respect level

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `격식체의 해라체` (2025-05-12).  
https://m.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=314653

**Evidence type**  
Official Korean-language advisory citing dictionary definitions.

**Supports**

- `격식체` is described as ceremonial/direct/assertive/objective and includes `해라체`, `하게체`, `하오체`, `하십시오체`.
- `비격식체` is described as softer/more subjective and includes `해체`, `해요체`.
- The formal/non-formal distinction therefore cannot be safely read as a simple high-respect/low-respect scale because both classes contain different addressee-honorification values.

**Does not support**

- `격식체` always being more appropriate in professional marketing;
- `비격식체` always being friendlier or more persuasive;
- a deterministic mapping from artifact type to style.

## [KOLA03] National Institute of Korean Language — one listener can receive formal/non-formal style shifting

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `해요체와 하게체를 섞어 쓰면 어색한가요?` (2026-06-12).  
https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=27&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=332226

**Evidence type**  
Current official Korean-language usage advisory.

**Supports**

- A speaker may commonly use formal and non-formal addressee styles with the same listener depending on the concrete situation.
- Use of `상대 높임법` depends on participant relationship and situation.
- Without context, a mixed formal/non-formal pattern cannot simply be declared wrong.

**Does not support**

- every mixed-style draft being natural or intentional;
- ignoring an approved organization/style rule;
- arbitrary switching among all six speech levels;
- a marketing-effect claim.

## [KOLA04] 2022 news-interview study — `하십시오체 ↔ 해요체` style shifting in formal situations

**Source**  
`뉴스 인터뷰를 활용한 한국어 상대 높임법 교육 방안 연구`, *학습자중심교과교육연구* 22(24), 2022, 885–902.  
https://scholar.kyobobook.co.kr/article/detail/4010036993180  
DOI: 10.22251/jlcci.2022.22.24.885

**Evidence type**  
Published Korean-language applied linguistics / Korean-language-education study.

**Supports**

- The study treats style shifting between `하십시오체` and `해요체` in formal situations as materially important to natural Korean use.
- Five university-published Korean textbooks and seven news-interview episodes with foreign interviewees were analyzed.
- The paper reports frequent `하십시오체 ↔ 해요체` shifting in news interviews and argues that a simple formal/non-formal dichotomy in textbooks is insufficient for natural relative-honorific competence.

**Does not support**

- every formal interview needing both styles;
- a universal frequency target for switching;
- marketing effectiveness;
- organization-specific style policy.

## [KOLA05] National Institute of Korean Language — subject, object, and addressee honorification are distinct mechanisms

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `높임표현의 유래` (2025-07-11).  
https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=261&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=318012

**Evidence type**  
Official Korean-language grammatical advisory citing dictionary definitions.

**Supports**

- `주체 높임법` honors the sentence subject, commonly through `-시-`.
- `객체 높임법` honors the target/object of an action, including modern lexical forms such as `뵙다`, `드리다`, `여쭈다`.
- `상대 높임법` honors or lowers the addressee through sentence endings.
- These are distinct grammatical dimensions and should not be collapsed into one politeness scalar.

**Does not support**

- a separate runtime unit for every Korean honorification mechanism;
- a hierarchy graph;
- any population or marketing preference.

## [KOLA06] National Institute of Korean Language — object honorification can coexist with an addressee-directed ending

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `상대 높임` (2024-12-02).  
https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=306904

**Evidence type**  
Official Korean-language grammatical advisory.

**Supports**

- In `이 책을 아주머니께 가져다 드려라`, lexical `드리다` realizes object honorification toward the action target.
- The clause separately contains a `해라체` sentence-ending realization toward the addressee.
- Object/action-target honorification and addressee speech level can therefore coexist in the same clause without being the same decision.

**Does not support**

- one universal rule for choosing `드리다`;
- automatic inference of speaker/listener hierarchy;
- a separate Korean object-honorification runtime owner.

## [KOLA07] KB customer-language guide — one organization uses different speech styles by communication function

**Source**  
KB의 생각, `고객의 신뢰를 얻는 KB고객언어 가이드`.  
https://kbthink.com/main/customerservice/kbcommunication.html

**Evidence type**  
Current first-party organization/customer-language policy.

**Supports**

- KB explicitly varies its tone by purpose, target, and situation.
- `하십시오체` is assigned to policy/notices, terms, objective data, expert/investment guidance, thanks, and error/refusal/restriction contexts.
- `해요체` is assigned to requests, recommendations, action prompts, difficult explanations, and asking customer intent.
- Nouns/nominal endings are used for titles, banners, notes, and buttons.
- One organization can legitimately resolve different speech styles by surface/function rather than one global customer relationship rule.

**Does not support**

- Korean consumers generally preferring this policy;
- banking-industry-wide rules;
- `policy → 하십시오체` or `CTA → 해요체` as universal mappings;
- marketing-effect claims.

## [KOLA08] Ahn, Lee & Chung 2015 — TV-ad formal/non-formal distribution and non-final sentences

**Source**  
안의진, 이철우, 정휘만 (2015), `TV광고언어에서 격식체와 비격식체의 사용빈도: 관여도, 문장유형, 발화맥락을 중심으로`, *한국광고홍보학보* 17(3), 232–260.  
https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002016155  
DOI: 10.16914/kjapr.2015.17.3.232

**Evidence type**  
KCI-indexed Korean advertising content analysis.

**Supports**

- The study analyzed 1,750 TV-ad sentences.
- Formal/non-formal ending frequencies differed by product involvement, sentence type, and articulation context in the sample.
- Formal styles appeared more often in declaratives/narration and non-formal styles more often in interrogative/imperative-like forms and drama in the sampled ads.
- 57.6% of analyzed TV-ad sentences were non-final constructions without a formal/non-formal sentence ending.
- Marketing surfaces can therefore legitimately contain no material speech-level decision at all.

**Does not support**

- a current universal Korean advertising norm;
- conversion or causal effectiveness;
- `high involvement → formal` as a prescriptive rule;
- transfer from TV ads to all channels.

## [KOLA09] Ahn 2018 — bounded consumer-response effect of formal/non-formal ad endings

**Source**  
안의진 (2018), `격식체, 비격식체 광고언어의 소비자 반응 효과`, *광고학연구* 29(5), 81–100.  
https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002368494  
DOI: 10.14377/KJA.2018.7.15.81

**Evidence type**  
KCI-indexed advertising experiment study.

**Supports**

- The paper reports two experiments comparing formal and non-formal Korean ad endings.
- In the tested conditions, non-formal endings produced more favorable attitudes toward the ad than formal endings.
- The reported favorability effect was larger for radio than print in the second experiment.
- Sentence-ending style can therefore materially affect audience response under bounded conditions.

**Does not support**

- `해요체` always outperforming formal endings;
- conversion, revenue, or purchase causality beyond the measured outcomes;
- a universal channel rule;
- a current population preference in 2026;
- a default speech-level recommendation for Marketing Practitioner.

## [KOLA10] National Institute of Korean Language — `사물존대` / indirect subject-honorification boundary

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `사물존칭인지 확인 부탁드립니다` (2025-10-20).  
https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=73&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=322418

**Evidence type**  
Official Korean-language grammatical usage advisory.

**Supports**

- In examples where `객실` is itself the subject, forms such as `객실이 없으세요`, `4인실이세요`, and `객실이 남아있으세요` are treated as inappropriate honorification of the room.
- Where the customer is the understood subject, forms with `-시-` may be appropriate.
- Indirect subject honorification may be analyzed when a body part, property, possession, or other closely related entity connects to the honored person.
- The scope of indirect honorification can involve grammatical disagreement.

**Does not support**

- `inanimate + -시- → always wrong`;
- `customer-related noun + -시- → always correct`;
- a customer-service correction lookup table;
- promotion of this mechanism as part of `KO-LANG-SPEECH-01`.

## [KOLA11] National Institute of Korean Language — `압존법` is not a general social/workplace hierarchy rule

**Source**  
국립국어원 온라인가나다, `압존법 관련 문의` (2026-06-26).  
https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=73&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=332830

**Evidence type**  
Current official Korean-language etiquette advisory citing `표준 언어 예절`.

**Supports**

- `압존법` is not fixed by Korean language regulations.
- `표준 언어 예절` describes traditional use mainly in family and teacher-student contexts.
- Applying it as a general broadcast, public-event, workplace, or social hierarchy rule cannot be assumed.

**Does not support**

- banning all `압존법`-like usage;
- a listener-rank/referent-rank algorithm;
- a hierarchy graph;
- any marketing-effect claim.
