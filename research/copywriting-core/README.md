# Copywriting Core Research

Status: **THEORY VERIFIED — BOUNDED RUNTIME INTEGRATED**

This directory preserves the substantive research lineage, theory freeze, and independent reviews establishing the **evidence-grounded copywriting decision system** within Chapter 04 of the Handbook.

The research resolves the traditional failure of LLM marketing copy—which vacillates between superficial formulaic slogans and untethered prose invention—by structuring copywriting into distinct, addressable decision stages: barrier diagnosis, angle/concept selection, argument progression, persuasive closure, sentence-level craft, and voice editing.

## Governing Question

> How should evidence-grounded message hierarchy, claim-proof coupling, and persuasive text realization be structured as addressable specialist knowledge without collapsing into an unconstrained text generator or a mandatory linear waterfall?

Key assumptions rejected during scoping:
- Copywriting is an undifferentiated creative step best prompted with generic tone guidelines.
- Literary eloquence or polished style is a valid proxy for strategic and empirical message validity.
- Every copywriting task must execute all stages from persuasion diagnosis to line editing sequentially.
